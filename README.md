# Laligurans Countryside Haven

Build a modern, mobile-responsive website for Laligurans Agro Solutions, an agro-tourism resort and cottage stay in Malarani-5, Bangi, Arghakhanchi, Nepal, set among terraced farmland and forested hillsides. The design should feel warm and rooted in place — the resort's own palette of wood tones, deep greens, and warm red/orange accents — with rich photography and a relaxed countryside feel. Avoid generic, cookie-cutter templates.

Pages & Sections

1. Home

Full-width hero image or slideshow (hillside views, cottages, evening bonfire)

"Laligurans Agro Solutions" name and a short tagline

Quick nav to Cottages, Facilities, Dining, Gallery, Contact

"Book Now" / "Contact Us" button

2. About

The resort's story as a working agro-farm and tourism stay

Emphasis on farm-to-table food, sustainable/local hospitality, and the natural setting

3. Location & Climate

Address: Malarani-5, Bangi, Arghakhanchi, Lumbini Province, Nepal — [Google Maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/3bZst6WvjZioSvXs6]

Set in Nepal's mid-hills, where Arghakhanchi's terrain ranges from warm lowland valleys to cooler subtropical slopes

Climate: lush, green monsoon season (June–September); clear, dry, cool winters (October–February) with the best mountain visibility; warm pre-monsoon spring (March–May)

Best time to visit: October–April for clear skies; June–September for the most vivid green scenery

Photos of the surrounding hills, farmland, and local wildlife (pheasants have been spotted on the grounds)

4. Cottages

Individually named cottages (e.g., "Cottage 1") along garden paths, lit with string lights and prayer flags in the evening

Photos of each cottage's exterior and interior

"Check Availability" button per cottage

5. Facilities & Amenities

Working vegetable garden/farm guests can walk through

Evening bonfire area

Garden swings and outdoor seating

Colorful ambient lighting across the grounds at night

Flower gardens (bougainvillea, chrysanthemum, and more)

On-site parking

6. Dining & Menu

Nepali thali / dal-bhat set (rice, dal, seasonal saag, tarkari, achar, papad)

Tandoori-style whole chicken

Chicken tikka/wings with mint-lemon dip and pickled salad

Grilled meat skewers with onion-carrot salad

Note: highlight ingredients sourced from the resort's own farm where possible

7. Gallery

Categorized photo grid: Scenery, Cottages, Dining, Evening Ambience, Farm & Garden

Lightbox/zoom on click

8. Contact & Booking

Contact form

Phone, email, social links

Embedded Google Maps

Booking inquiry form

Style Notes

Fully responsive across devices. Warm, earthy palette pulled from the resort's own wood/red/green tones. Smooth scrolling, subtle animation, fast-loading optimized images, clean and legible typography.

Content Notes

Use the resort's real photos throughout — a strong set is already available. Fill any remaining gaps with realistic placeholder text until final copy is ready.
